Validated and saved the submitted fix. The gameplay card generation no longer uses the directly predictable address/card-number-only Deck.deal output; instead, a game is initialized in two calls. The first call records the bet and commit block, and the second call, after a future block is mined, derives a per-game seed from block.blockhash(commitBlock + 1), the player, contract address, and bet. Cards are then produced from that seed plus the card index. This removes same-transaction/public-parameter determinism for the initial deal.

INVARIANTS USED:
Security invariant: card randomness used by active gameplay must not be computable solely from public player/cardNumber inputs at bet placement time. State invariant: a player cannot start an active Ongoing game until the delayed seed is available; pending deals reject additional ether and must wait until after commitBlock + 1. Bet invariant: the original wager is preserved through the pending phase and reused when initializing the Ongoing game. Range invariant: dealt cards remain constrained to uint8 values in [0, 51]. Gameplay invariant: existing payout and scoring paths are preserved after the delayed initial deal.
