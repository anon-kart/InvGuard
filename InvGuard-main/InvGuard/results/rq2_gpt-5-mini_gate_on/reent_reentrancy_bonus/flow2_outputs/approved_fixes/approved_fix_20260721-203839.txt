Added a reentrancy mutex (_locked) and moved state updates (setting claimedBonus and increasing rewardsForA) before the external call to withdrawReward in getFirstWithdrawalBonus. This enforces a checks-effects-interactions ordering and prevents reentrant calls to getFirstWithdrawalBonus while it is executing.

INVARIANTS USED:
1) Reentrancy guard invariant: _locked is false at entry and set to true during execution; any reentrant call will fail the require(!_locked).
2) Checks-Effects-Interactions: state changes (claimedBonus and rewardsForA) are performed before external calls, preventing reentrancy from observing stale state.
3) withdrawReward zeroes rewardsForA before performing the external call, ensuring funds cannot be drained twice in that call.
